# Juno-106 measurement

`juno106test.pd` plays a fixed sequence of 74 steps (about five minutes) into a Juno-106 or an
emulation of one: before each step the patch as Juno-106 SysEx (`F0 41 30 …`), then the notes on
MIDI channel 1. Record the synth's stereo output from before `start` to the end; nothing else is
needed. `juno106test.json` lists every step with its patch bytes and times, so a recording can be
compared with `juno~` rendering the same patches.

Steps: cutoff curve, resonance and self-oscillation, key follow, envelope depth and times, VCA
gate, the four high-pass positions, chorus off / I / II, LFO rate, depth and delay, VCF LFO,
pulse width, sub, noise, VCA level. Generated by `tools/make_juno106_test.py`.

Settings on the synth: effects other than the chorus off, portamento off, velocity off, poly
mode, bend range irrelevant. SysEx reception on (on a Juno-106: function switch III).
