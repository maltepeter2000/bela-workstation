# Juno-106 measurement

`juno106test.pd` plays a fixed sequence of 74 steps (about five minutes) into a Juno-106 or an
emulation of one: before each step the patch as Juno-106 SysEx (`F0 41 30 …`), then the notes on
MIDI channel 1. Record the synth's stereo output from before `start` to the end, either elsewhere or in Pd: with
`rec` on and Pd's audio input set to the synth (e.g. through BlackHole), `start` also writes
`juno106test-rec.wav` (stereo, 24 bit) next to the patch and stops it at the end. `juno106test.json` lists every step with its patch bytes and times, so a recording can be
compared with `juno~` rendering the same patches.

Steps: cutoff curve, resonance and self-oscillation, key follow, envelope depth and times, VCA
gate, the four high-pass positions, chorus off / I / II, LFO rate, depth and delay, VCF LFO,
pulse width, sub, noise, VCA level. Generated by `tools/make_juno106_test.py`.

Settings on the synth: effects other than the chorus off, portamento off, velocity off, poly
mode, bend range irrelevant. SysEx reception on (on a Juno-106: function switch III).

The synth must receive the SysEx: a DAW that drops SysEx on its way to plugins (Ableton Live)
will not do; use the instrument's standalone application with the MIDI port as its input.

`juno106test2.pd` is a second pass (57 steps, about five minutes): the filter frequency over the
whole cutoff range, key follow, envelope depth and VCF LFO measured on the self-oscillating
filter, the resonance threshold, the chorus on a sine, the bass end of the high-pass, and long
envelope and LFO times. Open only one of the two patches at a time; they share their receive
names.
